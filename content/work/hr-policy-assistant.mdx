## Context

Answering an HR policy question meant someone looking it up by hand in the policy documents. At Parkar Global Technologies I built a chatbot that answers those questions from the documents themselves, using retrieval-augmented generation.

## Architecture

<Diagram />

A question comes in through a FastAPI service, and LangChain coordinates the rest. FAISS semantic search finds the parts of the HR policy documents that matter for the question, and Llama inference writes the answer with them as context. It's written in Python.
